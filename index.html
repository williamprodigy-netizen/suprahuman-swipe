What matters

They warn you about the unknown number &mdash; steal this

The post-booking page says plainly: if a text arrives from an unknown number in the next hour, that is us, please reply. It converts a message that would normally be ignored into an expected one, and the reply itself is a micro-commitment that confirms the lead is reachable. We text booked leads from numbers they do not recognise and say nothing in advance.

The ICP is an identity, not a demographic

“Soft and out of shape former athletes” describes who someone used to be. It selects on self-image rather than age or gender, and it carries the promise inside it — you were this once, you can be again. Sharper than anything demographic.

Five minutes, stated up front

The video opens by committing to a five-minute runtime. Naming the cost of attention before asking for it is the opposite of the forced-consumption players elsewhere in this file, and it is a bet that respect converts better than capture.

Confirmation is a sequence, not a page

Booking is followed by a pre-call video, a checklist and an SMS confirmation. The same pattern as Her Closing Academy and Warrior Babe: three unrelated competitors all put work between booking and attending.
